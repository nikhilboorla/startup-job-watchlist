Daily screening rules

  • Company headcount: prioritize <500; mark the source and date, and exclude or label uncertain if not verifiable.
  • Location: DFW within 50 miles, then fully remote US positions that explicitly allow Texas.
  • Role family: Data Platform, AI Data, Data Infrastructure, Analytics Engineering, Databricks Platform, ML Data, Data/AI Infrastructure, Platform Automation.
  • Must-have skills: Python, PySpark/Spark, SQL, pipelines, orchestration, Databricks, Lakehouse, AI/GenAI data features.
  • Immigration: separate role-specific sponsorship from company H-1B history. Ask when PERM is initiated and whether I-140 premium processing is supported.
  • Equity: record type (RSU/options), number of shares, strike price, 409A/latest valuation, vesting/cliff, refreshes, dilution, lockup and liquidity path.
